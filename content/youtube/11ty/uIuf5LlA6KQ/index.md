This was a talk given at the 11ty International Symposium on Making Web Sites Real Good (2024): [https://conf.11ty.dev/2024/come-to-the-light-side-html-web-components/](https://conf.11ty.dev/2024/come-to-the-light-side-html-web-components/)

While Web Components have been around for years, they're seeing a bit of a renaissance thanks to an emerging approach for authoring them: ditch the shadow DOM and progressively enhance existing HTML.

In this talk, we'll look at what Web Components, how the &quot;HTML Web Component&quot; approach works, why it's awesome, and some tips, tricks, and gotchas when working with them.
