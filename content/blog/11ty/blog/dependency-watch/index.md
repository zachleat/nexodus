\<p>In our quest for smoother long term maintenance and hardened \<a href="https://www.11ty.dev/blog/npm-security/">security footprint\</a>, we wanted to let folks know that we’ve made even more exciting dependency simplification improvements on the \<a href="https://github.com/11ty/eleventy/releases/tag/v4.0.0-alpha.6">v4 canary prereleases\</a>!\</p> \<p>Notably when comparing \<code>v4.0.0-alpha.6\</code> to \<code>v3.1.2\</code>, we’ve:\</p> \<ul> \<li>Reduced our total dependency count from \<code>134\</code> to \<code>105\</code> (\<code class="numeric-down numeric-good">21.6%\</code>)\</li> \<li>Dropped third-party dependency count from \<code>123\</code> to \<code>89\</code> (\<code class="numeric-down numeric-good">27.6%\</code>)\</li> \<li>Decreased \<code>node\_modules\</code> weight from \<code>21 MiB\</code> to \<code>14 MiB\</code> (\<code class="numeric-down numeric-good">33.3%\</code>)\</li> \</ul> \<h2 id="full-history">Full History\</h2>
