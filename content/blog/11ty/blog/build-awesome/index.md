Eleventy v4 will be Build Awesome v4. I, \<a href="https://zachleat.com/">Zach\</a>, am still lovingly shepherding the open source project forward, the same as before.\</p> \<p>If you have a project \<em>using\</em> Eleventy, I can promise the same obsessive attention to smooth major-version upgrades. Folks have come to expect this from Eleventy, and I’m proud of the reputation we’ve earned there. We’ll continue to guide folks using the \<a href="https://www.11ty.dev/docs/plugins/upgrade-help/">Upgrade Helper plugin\</a>.\</p> \<p>Notably, \<strong>we are committed to keeping full compatibility with the existing Eleventy community and ecosystem\</strong>: that means you will be able to continue to use Eleventy plugins with Build Awesome!\</p> \<p>Further, we will even maintain compatibility with your existing Eleventy build commands. The proof will be in the pudding, and I look forward to delighting folks here.\</p> \<h2 id="what-does-build-awesome-pro-mean-for-eleventy">What does Build Awesome Pro mean for Eleventy?\</h2> \<p>After the launch of Font Awesome Pro, the number of free icons in the Font Awesome library \<em>tripled\</em>, and the full set grew from 675 icons to just over 64,000 icons!\</p> \<p>After the launch of Web Awesome Pro, the project has now shipped with over 50 components, 30 reusable patterns, and a whole slew of themes with light and dark modes!\</p> \<p>Build Awesome Pro means that we can continue to grow and invest in the open source project, providing more features, more maintenance, and increased stability for \<em>everyone\</em>.\</p> \<p>Font Awesome Pro is not required to use Font Awesome. Web Awesome Pro is not required to use Web Awesome. And Build Awesome Pro will not be required to use Build Awesome (Eleventy).\</p> \<p>The Awesomeverse of projects have a straightforward business model and Font Awesome has the track record to prove it. You subscribe and you get more: more services, more support — all while helping to grow the free and open source version for everyone. No shenanigans.\</p> \<h2 id="what-does-this-mean-for-the-open-collective-account">What does this mean for the Open Collective account?\</h2> \<p>For a bit of history, you can read about our Open Collective fundraising push in early 2024 in the blog post: \<a href="https://www.zachleat.com/web/symbiotic-open-source/">\<em>Open Source Needs to be Financially Symbiotic\</em>\</a>, which has some notes about our current Open Collective account. We also made a change last year to \<a href="https://www.zachleat.com/web/permanent-eleventy-facepile/">permanently feature individuals that have contributed to Eleventy\</a> over the years in our site’s (very large) backer facepile.\</p> \<p>This account is still being used to pay for expenses related to the project: costs related to our web site, expenses for the Meetup (Zoom subscriptions), honorariums and other costs from the 11ty Conference (in 2024), as well as some recurring donations to some dependencies in our stack. If you have eligible expenses, please use the \<a href="https://opencollective.com/11ty">Submit Expense button on Open Collective\</a> to get reimbursed!\</p> \<p>This also includes hosting and domain costs for community resources!
