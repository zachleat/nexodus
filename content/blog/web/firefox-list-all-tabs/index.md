\<p>I am a habitual tab hoarder so I created a Firefox addon to help me manage every one of my very special browser tabs.\</p> \<p>\<img src="https://www.zachleat.com/img/built/lafT3xBfoz-2972.avif" alt="Screenshot of List All Tabs (light mode) showing 306 open tabs (137 active and 169 unloaded)" loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="2972" height="2138">\</p> \<p>Sort tabs by oldest, unload them, find duplicates, and consolidate tabs to a single window — buncha useful stuff in there!\</p> \<ul> \<li>\<a href="https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/list-all-tabs-updated/">Download from Mozilla Addons\</a>\</li> \<li>\<a href="https://github.com/zachleat/list-all-tabs">Source code on GitHub\</a>\</li> \</ul> \<h2 id="features">Features\</h2> \<ul> \<li>Go directly to a tab by clicking on it.\</li> \<li>Shows Duplicate tabs (any tab opened more than once)\</li> \<li>A special section to show any tabs currently playing audio (video counts if not muted) (new in v1.5)\</li> \<li>Supports Multi-account containers (and shows with the color of the container too)\</li> \<li>Search/filter tabs by title, URL or container name.\</li> \<li>Sort tabs by tab order, least recently used or most recently used.\</li> \<li>Full Undo and redo support (new in v1.5)\</li> \<li>A new purgatory-style Saved section to close tabs you may want to restore later (new in v1.5)\</li> \<li>Toggles to show/hide Unloaded and Pinned tabs.\</li> \<li>\<em>Merge\</em> consolidates all a group’s tabs into a new window.\</li> \<li>Tabs you haven’t used for a week or more show a stale time difference (e.g. \<code>2w\</code> or \<code>3mo\</code>).\</li> \<li>Light and dark modes.\</li> \<li>Pinned tabs are denoted and listed last (by design, pinned tabs are indestructible and cannot be operated on).\</li> \<li>\<code>about:blank\</code> tabs are ignored.\</li> \</ul>
