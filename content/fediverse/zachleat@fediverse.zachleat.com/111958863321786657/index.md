New web component: \<throb-ber>

Adds a small rainbow gradient overlay until all of the images nested inside have finished loading. Works best with expensive dynamically generated images (like dynamic screenshot images).

📮 Blog post: [https://www.zachleat.com/web/throbber/](https://www.zachleat.com/web/throbber/)
