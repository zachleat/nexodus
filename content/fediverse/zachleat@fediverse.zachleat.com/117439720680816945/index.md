I am a habitual tab hoarder (I have 321 tabs open right now) so I created a Firefox addon to help me manage every one of my very special tabs:

[https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/list-all-tabs-updated/](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/list-all-tabs-updated/)

You can sort your tabs by oldest, unload them, duplicate hosts, consolidate tabs to a single window — buncha useful stuff in there
