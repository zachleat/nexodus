TIL \`hyphens: auto\` does not add hyphens for capitalized words (in English at least).

extraordinarily (wraps with hyphen)  
Extraordinarily (no wrap no hyphen)

Adding \`word-break: break-word\` breaks the capitalized word, but still no hyphen 🤨

Demo (applies to Firefox and Chrome, working in Safari): [https://codepen.io/zachleat/pen/myeYbJV](https://codepen.io/zachleat/pen/myeYbJV)
