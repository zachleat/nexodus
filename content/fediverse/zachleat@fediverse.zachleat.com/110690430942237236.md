If I understand correctly, a “true threat” changed from how a reasonable person would interpret violent speech and raised the bar that the speaker must have a conscious disregard for how violent speech would be interpreted.

It seems unlikely that this change will help victims of harassment—but it almost certainly creates more protection for harassers.
