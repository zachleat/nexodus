Worth remembering that Next.js internally bundles 136MB of dependencies in its package output (see [https://npmx.dev/package-code/next/v/16.4.3/dist%2Fcompiled](https://npmx.dev/package-code/next/v/16.4.3/dist%2Fcompiled)) so even though an npm audit (on v16.4.3) reports 0 vulnerabilities, it actually has 32 unreported (some might argue hidden) vulnerabilities.
