If you want your CSS custom properties to be available in \`::backdrop\` or \`::selection\`, note that they do not inherit!

Use \`:root, ::backdrop, ::selection {}\` to declare custom property values, suggests [@Kilian](https://mastodon.social/@Kilian):  
[https://kilianvalkhof.com/2023/css-html/root-isnt-global/](https://kilianvalkhof.com/2023/css-html/root-isnt-global/)
